TYLY Weekly Flash

Net sales (demand, USD after discounts)

  • LY is matched on fiscal week and weekday (for example, Wed of W30 vs Wed of W30), not on calendar date.
  • No YoY at day level: about 35 transactions a day in this sample, so a single-day comparison is mostly noise.
  • Period to date starts at W23, the first week in the extract. A full fiscal YTD would need W1–W22.
  • "Where it came from" splits the YoY into percentage points: (TY − LY sales of a segment) ÷ total LY sales. Gen Z and new customers overlap, so the split uses both at once; the four cells add up to the YoY (±0.1 pp rounding). A negative cell means that segment sold less than LY. Week-to-date segments are small, so read them as directional.
  • New = first-ever purchase with the brand; repeat = Retained or Reactivated, both from COHORT_STATUS at the time of purchase.
  • Counts are distinct customers in the window. Share changes are in percentage points (pp).
